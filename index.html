PROJECT Z

THE GREEN ZONE

DEMAND ENGINE
The Green Zone consumes Medicine, Fuel, Food and Ammo.
Survivors travel toward the highest-value opportunity.
Deliveries increase stock and lower prices.
As stock runs low, prices rise and attract more Survivors.
PLAYER ECOSYSTEM
Some Survivors naturally clash with each other.
● RUNNER + ● RAIDER
or ● RAIDER + ● ENFORCER
create ENCOUNTERS when they meet.
DYNAMIC THREAT
ENCOUNTERS generate local infection ☣
Infection persists and slowly dissipates over time.
Overrun hotspots become lethal for Survivors 💀
