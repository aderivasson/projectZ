PROJECT Z

THE GREEN ZONE

DEMAND ENGINE CREATES TRAFFIC
The Green Zone consumes Medicine, Fuel, Food and Ammo.
As stocks fall, prices rise.
Survivors travel toward the highest-value opportunities.
Deliveries increase stocks and lower prices.
PREDATOR ECOSYSTEM CREATES ACTIVITY
Some Survivors become RENEGADES and prey on other Survivors.
When RENEGADES and other Survivors meet, they create FIGHTS.
Activity emerges around valuable opportunities.
DYNAMIC THREAT CREATES TENSION
FIGHTS generate local infection ☣
Infection persists and slowly dissipates over time.
Infected hotspots push Survivors away.
Tension reshapes traffic across the Green Zone.
