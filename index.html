THE GREEN ZONE

DEMAND ENGINE

The Green Zone consumes Medicine, Fuel, Food and Ammo.

● ● ● Survivors automatically travel to the resource that is currently the most valuable.

Successful deliveries increase stock and lower the price.

As stock runs low, prices rise and attract more Survivors.

PLAYER ECOSYSTEM

Encounters between ● RUNNER + ● RAIDER or ● RAIDER + ● ENFORCER create activity.

DYNAMIC THREAT

When activity is detected, ☣ Infected groups converge on the hotspot and try to overwhelm Survivors.

⚪ Survivors who fail to escape are lost before returning to the Green Zone.
